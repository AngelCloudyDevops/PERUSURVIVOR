# Arte ambiental v2

Palomas con cuello verde/violeta, barras en las alas, ojo naranja y patas rosadas; cuatro poses (reposo, picoteo, alas arriba y abajo). Tres arbustos con siluetas distintas y flores o bayas, más una mata de ichu para Machu Picchu. Estilo cartoon con acabado pixel y contorno oscuro, sin sustituir césped ni árboles.

Generado mediante la herramienta integrada image_gen. Original: `public/assets/environment/nature-atlas-v2.png`; ejecución: `public/assets/environment/nature-atlas-v2.webp`. El PNG se conserva para edición, pero el build distribuye solamente WebP. `scripts/pack-nature-art.py` empaqueta mecánicamente por vecino más cercano en ocho celdas de 128 px; `nature-art-audit.json` registra hashes, tamaño y límites. El atlas compartido requiere una llamada drawImage por elemento; mantiene los cupos de seis aves y 32 plantas reactivas, sin capas ni partículas nuevas. Las sombras se dibujan separadas y ancladas al suelo.

## Prompt de generación

Create ONE production-ready transparent 2D GAME SPRITE ATLAS, landscape 2:1 aspect ratio, EXACTLY FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows, 8 equal square cells. NO cell borders, NO labels, NO ground, NO cast shadows, transparent empty background. Each sprite fully isolated with at least 12% transparent margin to each cell boundary. All sprites centered horizontally and their feet/base aligned at 80% of their respective cell height. Style: refined hand-drawn cartoon game sprites with clean dark outlines, crisp pixel finish, compact clustered shading, 3/4 overhead RPG perspective like detailed chibi survival game characters; NOT low-detail geometric placeholders, NOT photorealism, NOT 3D. Limited colors, coherent daylight upper left, no blur. Upper row is SAME small Lima rock pigeon facing right in FOUR animation poses: column1 standing idle; column2 bending head to peck; column3 airborne with wings raised; column4 airborne wings lowered. Pigeon has rounded blue-gray breast, small expressive orange eye, iridescent emerald/purple neck, two dark wing bars, layered feathers, pink feet, short beak. Same body scale across four poses, full wings within cell. Lower row: column1 asymmetric rounded leafy park bush with layered organic leaves, warm olive highlights and tiny white flowers; column2 different low wide park bush with glossy sage/forest green leaves and small pale yellow blooms; column3 irregular three-lobed leafy park shrub with sparse reddish berries; column4 dense Andean ichu tussock, elegant curved sage and straw-colored blades and fluffy golden seedheads, organic fan shape. Vegetation should have clear curved silhouette, visible individual grouped leaf clusters, dark green outline and layered two/three-tone cartoon shading matching the character style. Fill 65-75% cell width for bushes, 45-60% for idle birds and 75% for flying birds. This replaces crude rectangle birds and geometric shrubs in a Peru cartoon survival game. No additional objects, text or frame lines.

El paquete de publicación excluye los cuatro atlas ambientales antiguos desactivados por simpleStyle; sus originales siguen en Git. Al reactivar ese modo, el build los vuelve a incluir. Así se conserva margen de tamaño sin reducir la calidad del atlas nuevo.
