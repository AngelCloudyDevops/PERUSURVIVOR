# Bancas y faroles

Dos bancas cartoon con acabado pixel: madera miel y hierro curvado en Campo de Marte, madera rojiza y metal ornamental en Plaza de Armas. Farol colonial con poste labrado, vidrio y base apoyada en el suelo. Mantener siluetas claras, perspectiva ligeramente elevada, contornos oscuros y sombras pequeñas al pie. Se conservan coordenadas, colisiones, cantidades y orden de actores.

Un atlas compartido de 512 × 256, cargado bajo demanda. Una llamada de imagen por banca o farol estable; dos por farol únicamente durante el encendido gradual. La versión nocturna se prepara en un único canvas al cargar, iluminando solo los paneles del vidrio. Reutiliza DayNight y su luz del suelo; no añade gradientes por objeto, partículas, temporizadores ni entidades. Pausa y arenas mantienen el comportamiento previo del ciclo. Si falla el recurso, se conserva el dibujo anterior.

Generación con la herramienta integrada image_gen. Original: `public/assets/environment/street-furniture-v1.png`. Recurso: `public/assets/environment/street-furniture-v1.webp`. El build omite el PNG duplicado. Empaquetado mecánico por vecino más cercano y espacio transparente: `scripts/pack-street-furniture.py`; hashes y límites en `street-furniture-audit.json`.

## Prompt de generación

Create ONE transparent production-ready 2D GAME SPRITE SHEET with THREE isolated street furniture objects, arranged horizontally in THREE clearly separated equal columns. Wide landscape 3:1 canvas. Each object fully inside its own column with 10% transparent margin, no overlap, no text, no labels, no grid. All front-facing, with a subtly elevated three-quarter overhead RPG game perspective showing seat/top surfaces, parallel verticals. Refined CARTOON drawing with crisp pixel finish, clean dark outlines, carefully grouped cel shading, warm daylight upper left, matching detailed chibi game characters and ornate colonial Lima fountain/facade sprites. NOT photorealism, NOT 3D, NOT crude rectangular placeholders. FIRST COLUMN: a beautiful park bench, warm honey-brown wooden slats with visible grain, elegant dark green/black wrought iron curved armrests and scroll supports, four sturdy feet, broad low silhouette, view of backrest and seat from slightly above. SECOND COLUMN: a distinct colonial plaza bench, richer reddish walnut slats, more ornate cast iron end supports with tasteful curled floral metalwork, curved armrests, sturdy feet, broad low silhouette. Both benches same width and scale, proportions roughly 2:1 width to height. THIRD COLUMN: one tall single-lantern colonial Peruvian street lamp, dark bronze/black fluted slender pole, ornate cast iron foot touching the baseline, graceful moldings, a six-sided glass lantern with a tiny finial and decorative curved cap. Glass should be cool pale blue-gray and clearly unlit, with a visible simple inner bulb, no glow. Lantern has a visible broad front glass pane with angled side panes. Lamp full height with all parts connected, no detached metalwork. Make the furniture charming and crafted, readable grouped details rather than texture noise. No ground, no cast shadow, no glow, no plants, no architecture, no characters, no signs or flags. Real transparent background. Objects completely uncropped.
